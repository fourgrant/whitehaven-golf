Commish Guide

Step-by-step instructions for running a weekly round from start to finish.

Overview

Whitehaven runs a 9-hole shambles format. Each week follows five steps: send the RSVP link, set up the round, build teams, enter scores, then finalize. Scores and round state auto-save to the cloud as you work — safe to close and reopen at any point.

0 RSVP → 1 Round → 2 Teams → 3 Scores → 4 Finalize

📋 Step 0 — Send the RSVP Link

Before round day
  1. Go to the Round tab and save the round (date, course, buy-in).
  2. A green RSVP Link card appears — click Copy Link and paste it into the group chat.
  3. Players open the link, pick their name from the dropdown, and submit. The link automatically closes after midnight the night before the round.
On round day

Go to the Teams tab. An RSVPs card at the top shows everyone who committed. You have two options:

  • Import RSVPs — checks in every RSVP'd player with one click.
  • Copy List — copies a numbered list of names and contacts formatted for a group text (e.g. to confirm headcount before you arrive).
First-time players — if a player doesn't have an email/phone on file, the RSVP form asks for it and saves it automatically. Next time they RSVP, those fields are skipped.

⛳ Step 1 — Round Tab

What to do
  1. Set the Date and Course.
  2. Confirm the Buy-in — default is $12 all-in per player.
  3. Click Save Round & Go to Teams.
Buy-in breakdown ($12 default)
Bucket Amount Notes
CTH Pool $2 $1 per par-3 hole (holes 2 & 5)
Main Pool $10 75% team win, 25% skins — or 100% team win if no skins hit

👥 Step 2 — Teams Tab

Check in players

If you used the RSVP link, click Import RSVPs at the top to check everyone in at once. Otherwise, tap each player's name to check them in manually.

You can also use the Paste List button to bulk-check-in players by pasting a list of names — handy when you have a pre-confirmed lineup in a text thread.

Active vs. Inactive players — once the league has 8+ tracked rounds, the check-in list splits into two sections. Active Players (played in the last 8 rounds) appear at the top. Inactive Players are collapsed below — click expand ▼ to reveal them. Inactive players are fully selectable; they just haven't shown up recently.

Assign teams

You have three options:

  • Auto-Balance — click ⚖ Auto-Balance to split players into even teams based on scoring averages. Teams are always 3- or 4-man (or a mix), distributed as evenly as the headcount allows. Best for most weeks.
  • Shuffle — click 🔀 Shuffle to re-roll teams. Same checked-in players, different combinations, balance preserved by skill tier. Click again until you like the look of it.
  • Manual — use each player's dropdown to assign them to a team letter (A, B, C…). Use this when you want full control.
Play-together requests

Sometimes a few players want to be on the same team (e.g. visiting friends, a couple, a buddy group). Use Play-Together Requests:

  1. Check in everyone first.
  2. Click + Add Pairing, tick 2 or more players, click Add Pairing.
  3. Click ⚖ Auto-Balance or 🔀 Shuffle — the algorithm assigns each pairing to the same team and balances around them.

Heads-up: pairing strong players together limits how fairly the rest can be balanced. The algorithm compensates as best it can, but a stack of low-handicap players on one team will likely have a small edge. Use sparingly when fairness matters.

A live team preview shows the groups as you assign. When everything looks right, click Lock In & Open Scores.

Guest players — use the + Add Guest button to add a walk-up player who isn't in the roster. Guests are included in team math for the day but don't affect the permanent stats leaderboard.
Shared across devices — check-ins and team letters auto-save to the cloud as you go. Any commish on any device sees the same draft round on refresh, so if a no-show forces a reshuffle on the first tee, anyone with the commish password can adjust teams. Clear Round only forgets the round on the device you click it from; another commish device still has it.

🏌️ Step 3 — Scores Tab

Individual scores

Enter each player's total stroke count for the 9 holes in the field next to their name. These are used to update player scoring averages — they do not determine the winning team. Scores auto-save to the cloud as you type (no Save button needed).

Team shamble score

At the top of each team card there is a gold-bordered box — this is where you enter the team's combined shamble score. The team with the lowest score wins the team pot. Enter this manually (it's the actual shamble result from the course, not calculated from individual scores). Team scores also auto-save.

Tiebreaker

If two or more teams end up with the same shamble score, a Tiebreaker card appears automatically. Enter each tied team's score on hole 9 — lowest wins. If still tied, hole 8 appears, then hole 7, and so on until a winner is determined. Golf scores (including negatives) are supported. The tiebreaker result is saved and shown in the round's History card.

Skins

In the Skins section, use the dropdown for each hole (1–9) to select the hole winner, or leave it as No winner. A skin is only awarded when one player clearly wins the hole outright — ties carry over per your group's rules.

If no skins are claimed at all, the full main pool goes to the winning team instead.

Closest to the Pin (Holes 2 & 5)

Holes 2 and 5 are the par-3s. In the CTH section, pick the player who landed closest to the pin on each hole. Each CTH winner claims $1 × the number of players from the CTH pool.

Everything auto-saves. Scores, team totals, skins, CTPs, and tiebreaker data all save to the cloud as you enter them. You can close the app mid-round and pick up exactly where you left off. The payout table also updates live.

💰 Step 4 — Review Payouts & Finalize

Track cash

The payout table shows every player's winnings broken out by team win, skins, and CTH. Use the Paid In and Paid Out checkboxes to track who has settled up as you collect and distribute cash.

Share with the group

Click Copy Payout Summary to copy a plain-text summary to your clipboard — ready to paste into a group chat.

Finalize the round

When all scores and payouts are confirmed, click ✓ Finalize Round. This will:

  • Save the round permanently to the database.
  • Update each player's scoring average (last 10 rounds).
  • Archive the round to the History tab for everyone to view.
  • Clear the active round from your browser.
This cannot be undone. Finalized rounds can be deleted from History, but deletion is permanent and will reverse the stat updates for all players in that round.

🗂 Players Tab — Roster Management

The Players tab is separate from the weekly round flow — use it any time to manage the roster.

  • Add player — type their name and click Add. They'll appear at check-in immediately.
  • Edit contact info — click Edit next to any player to set or update their email and phone number. This is the info used for RSVP pre-fill and email reminders. Players who've RSVP'd at least once will already have this on file.
  • Deactivate — players sitting out the season can be deactivated so they don't clutter the check-in list. Their history and averages are preserved.
  • Reactivate — bring them back at any time with one click.
  • Recalculate all stats — rebuilds every player's average and round count from their finalized rounds. Run it once after the scoring fix, or any time the leaderboard looks off.

📊 How Scoring Averages Work

  • Each player's average is the mean of their last 10 finalized rounds played in the app — the exact rounds shown when you tap their name on the Stats tab. The two always match.
  • Players need 5+ tracked rounds to appear in the ranked leaderboard; until then they sit in the "Needs 5+ rounds to rank" section.
  • Averages come from app-tracked rounds only — older spreadsheet numbers are placeholders that get replaced as soon as a player logs finalized rounds.
  • Auto-Balance uses these averages to create balanced teams, so keeping scores accurate each week matters.
  • Individual scores (entered per player in the Scores tab) feed the averages — the team shamble score does not.
  • If the numbers ever look off, use Recalculate all stats on the Players tab to rebuild every average and round count from finalized rounds.
  • After finalizing a round, the Stats leaderboard shows a small ▲ or ▼ next to each participant's rank — green ▲ means they moved up, red ▼ means they dropped. Only players from the most recent round are marked, and only if they had 5+ rounds before it.

Quick Reference

Question Answer
What if I close the browser mid-round? Everything auto-saves to the cloud as you enter it. Reopen the app on any device and continue exactly where you left off.
Two teams are tied — now what? A Tiebreaker card appears automatically. Enter hole 9 scores for each tied team — lowest wins. If still tied, hole 8 appears, then 7, etc. Negative scores are fine.
What if nobody wins a skin? Leave all skins as No winner. The full main pool rolls to the winning team automatically.
Can I change scores after finalizing? No — delete the round from History and re-enter if a correction is needed. All stat updates reverse on delete.
What's the difference between team score and individual scores? Team score (gold box) is the shamble result — it determines the winner. Individual scores feed each player's personal average.
How do I share a specific round result? Click the 🔗 button on any History card to copy a direct link to that round. Opening it takes anyone straight to the expanded results.
Which holes are par 3s? Holes 2 and 5. CTH is only contested on these two holes.
How much does each CTH winner get? $1 × number of players checked in, drawn from the $2-per-player CTH pool.
How do I update a player's email or phone? Go to the Players tab, find the player, and click Edit. Players who've RSVP'd before already have their info saved.

Release Notes

Running log of changes to the Whitehaven Golf League app. Newest first.

v1.7 June 1, 2026

Leaderboard Scoring Fix — Averages Now Match a Player's Rounds

  • Averages are now computed only from finalized rounds played in the app. A player's leaderboard average is the mean of their last 10 finalized rounds — exactly the rounds you see when you tap their name. The old spreadsheet seed numbers were placeholders that never reconciled with a player's actual scores; they're now replaced as soon as rounds are tracked.
  • Fixed a double-count bug. Finalizing a round used to count that round twice in the average. It's now counted exactly once.
  • Finalize and delete now agree. Both paths recompute stats the same way, so a player's round count no longer changes depending on which action ran last.
  • Player popup shows finalized rounds only. In-progress rounds no longer sneak into the history list, so the scores shown always average out to the displayed number.
  • New "Recalculate all stats" button on the Players tab corrects every player's average and round count in one tap.
  • Heads up: with averages now app-only, players with fewer than 5 tracked rounds sit in the "Needs 5+ rounds to rank" section until they play more.
v1.6 May 10, 2026

Shared Draft Rounds — Multi-Device Team Builder

  • Active rounds now sync across devices. The draft round you're setting up no longer lives only on the device that created it — any commish on any phone or laptop sees the same check-ins and team letters on refresh.
  • Auto-save during setup. Checking a player in, picking a team letter, running Auto-Balance, or shuffling all save instantly to the cloud. A small Saving… / Saved ✓ indicator confirms each write.
  • First-tee reshuffles. If a player doesn't show up, any commish can uncheck them and re-balance — the change appears for everyone else after a refresh.
  • Save Teams button renamed to Lock In & Open Scores to match its new role: setup is already saved, the button just opens the round for score entry.
v1.5 May 3, 2026

Auto-Balance Always Produces 3- or 4-Man Teams

  • Fixed a bug where Auto-Balance and Shuffle could leave a team with only 2 players when a Play-Together pairing was active. Teams are now guaranteed to be either 3- or 4-man, with sizes distributed as evenly as possible across the field.
  • Example: 18 checked-in players now always produces sizes 4-4-4-3-3 (never 4-4-4-4-2).
v1.4 May 3, 2026

Player Activity States — Leaderboard & Team Builder

  • Active vs. Inactive — Players who haven't played in any of the last 8 rounds are now classified as inactive. This keeps the main leaderboard focused on current regulars.
  • Stats page: Inactive players collapse into a new section below the "Needs 5+ rounds" group. Click expand ▼ to see them with a "last played N rounds ago" label. Once the league crosses 25 total tracked rounds, inactive players are hidden from the public leaderboard entirely.
  • Team Builder: The check-in list is now grouped into Active Players and Inactive Players sections. Inactive players are collapsed by default but fully selectable — the commish can still check in anyone who shows up.
  • The footer on the Stats page now reflects the active activity rule automatically.
v1.3 April 27, 2026

Leaderboard Rank Change Indicators & Compact Season Awards

  • ▲/▼ arrows on the leaderboard show whether each player moved up or down in the rankings after the most recent round — only visible for players who participated in that round.
  • Green ▲ means your average improved and your rank went up; red ▼ means your rank dropped. No arrow means no change or you didn't play.
  • Season Awards tiles now show 1st place only by default — tap More ▼ on any tile to reveal 2nd and 3rd place.
v1.2 April 25, 2026

Team Shuffle & Play-Together Pairings

  • 🔀 Shuffle button on the Teams tab re-rolls team compositions while keeping balance comparable. Click again until the matchup feels right.
  • Play-Together Requests let you lock 2+ players onto the same team before balancing. Auto-Balance and Shuffle both honor pairings.
  • Strong players paired together will produce a slight team advantage by design — the algorithm balances around the constraint as best it can.
  • Cache-busting added to deploys so future updates don't get stuck behind a stale browser cache.
v1.1 April 22, 2026

Leaderboard Fixes & Polish

  • Fixed leaderboard tile name display.
  • Best Round superlative now restricted to round winners only.
  • Tied 2nd / 3rd place rows on the podium now show all tied names.
  • Added an SVG favicon.
